# Viral topics — storytelling — 2026-11-22

Researcher Agent, first run. Sources are mostly marketing blogs and vendor articles, so treat claims as directional.
No per-video view counts were retrieved this run, so every metric reads "unknown". The competitor-analyst agent can fill those in.

## What the market is saying (Nov 2026)
- Raw, authentic, story-driven clips beat polished edits; the first seconds still have to hook ([Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/2026-social-media-trends)).
- Recurring **series / "shows"** beat one-off clips ([Slate](https://slateteams.com/blog/social-media-trends-2026)).
- Winning formats: POV, faceless explainers, tight cuts + on-screen text + a payoff ([Revid](https://www.revid.ai/blog/short-form-video-trends)).
- Short Reels with fast hooks top Instagram reach; Reels + carousels pair well for storytelling ([New Engen](https://newengen.com/insights/instagram-trends/), [mean.ceo](https://blog.mean.ceo/instagram-trends-november-2026/)).
- Small accounts (< 5k followers) reportedly get the most views per video on YouTube Shorts ([Revid](https://www.revid.ai/blog/short-form-video-trends)).
- Top faceless story sub-niches: scary/true crime, Reddit-thread narration, moral tales/fables, Stoicism, history/sleep stories ([Fliki](https://fliki.ai/blog/best-faceless-youtube-niches), [easyviral](https://easyviral.ai/blog/faceless-tiktok-niches), [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/faceless-youtube-channels/)).
- Founder stories under 60 s, told messy and honest, are the strongest small-brand lane ([Under30CEO](https://www.under30ceo.com/short-form-video-founder-content-2026/), [Tolstoy](https://www.gotolstoy.com/blog/founder-story-examples)).
