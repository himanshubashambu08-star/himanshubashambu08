# Editing Style

Written rules and preferences for how your videos should be edited.

**What goes here**
- A style guide (e.g. `style-guide.md`) covering:
  - Pacing and cut frequency
  - Caption style (font, size, position, colours, animation)
  - Transitions and effects you use (and ones to avoid)
  - Music and sound-effect preferences
  - Colour grading / LUTs
  - Aspect ratios and video length per platform
- Presets, LUT files, or templates from your editing software
